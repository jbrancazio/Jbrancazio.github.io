title: Coffeebar Staffing Model
summary: My first data project, before I knew to call it that. I used drink-by-drink sales to work out how many people a busy coffeebar actually needed.
years: "Intelligentsia, Venice"
stack: [Excel, POS data]
era: earlier
order: 4
---

When I took over Intelligentsia's Venice Coffeebar it had 12 staff and struggled with service and wait times.

I downloaded every drink sale from the register system and averaged it in 30-minute blocks. Then I set a rule for how many drinks one person could handle, which mapped out the staff every hour of the week needed. It showed we needed 21 people, not 12, and it gave me the numbers to make that case. Over my time running the store its bottom line grew 150%.

The spreadsheet became the company standard. It was promptly replaced by real software that did what I had written, better and in real time. It was still a fun project to work on.
