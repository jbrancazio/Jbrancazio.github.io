A co-worker and I noticed a problem in our hiring numbers. People stayed about a year and a half on average, but it took two full years to be fully trained. Most people left before they finished.

We wrote a proposal to corporate. Train the skills needed for daily work first, as fast as possible, then offer everything else as optional learning with pay incentives. After it worked in our store, we took it to the nine other stores, adapted it for each, and it became the company standard. Barista ramp time improved by 83%.

That led to writing the manager-in-training and educator-in-training programs. Half of the manager curriculum went to communication, leadership, and service, because the company was known for great coffee and not for great service.
